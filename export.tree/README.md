This database includes the raw and preprocessed task-based functional scans reported in Diveica et al. (2026, PNAS). 

The associated anatomical and resting-state scans are available at: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds003592 .

The associated scripts and analysis outputs can be accessed via the Open Science Framework: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/BT8ZF .

The associated group-level activation maps can be visualized via NeuroVault: https://identifiers.org/neurovault.collection:17919 .
